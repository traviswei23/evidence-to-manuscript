For a centered aperture in the ideal Gaussian model, the collected-power
fraction reached 86.47\% at $a=w$ with 200 radial cells.
At the four radii tested, the numerical and analytic integrals differed
by less than 0.002 percentage points. This fraction alone cannot identify
the optimal aperture for a receiver exposed to background radiation.

For a centered aperture in the ideal Gaussian model, the numerical method
gave a fraction of collected power of 86.47\% when $a=w$
using 200 radial cells. At the four radii tested, the difference from
the analytic integral was less than 0.002 percentage points.
The value is a collected-power fraction. It does not determine an
optimal aperture for a receiver with background radiation.
The published study reports the spatial power distribution on a receiving
plane for different sea surface models \cite{Gao2025}.
